# Privacy Policy for DIU Routine

**Effective Date:** September 8, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to DIU Routine. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data when you use the DIU Routine application ("App"). The App is developed and maintained by Arik Sadman. We believe in building a privacy-first application, which means the App is designed to run completely on your device without collecting, storing, or transmitting your personal data to any developer servers.

## 2. Information the App Collects
The App **does not** collect any personally identifiable information. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or device identifiers. There are no accounts, and no registration is required to use the App.

## 3. Information Stored Locally
All of your personal preferences, including your theme, accent color, selected UI density, custom courses, and planner data (tasks and exams), are stored **strictly locally** on your device using Capacitor Preferences. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us. 

## 4. Network Requests
The App makes limited network requests strictly for core functionality:
- **Routine Data:** The App fetches the latest class routine data directly from a public, developer-maintained GitHub repository (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/arik-sadman313/diu-routine-data/main/latest.json`).
- **Weather Data:** If you use the weather feature, the App fetches weather forecasts from Open-Meteo. 

## 5. Routine Data
The routine data provided in the App is maintained by the developer. The App downloads this data to your device for offline use. You cannot upload or transmit routine data from the App to our servers.

## 6. Weather Data
If you enable the weather feature and grant location permissions, the App will use your device's geolocation to request weather data from the Open-Meteo API (`api.open-meteo.com`). Open-Meteo is a privacy-friendly weather service. Your location is sent only to Open-Meteo to fetch the forecast and is not tracked by the App. If you prefer not to use geolocation, you can manually search for a city, which will securely query the Open-Meteo Geocoding API.

## 7. Notifications
The App uses local device notifications to remind you of upcoming classes or planner events. These notifications are scheduled and triggered entirely on your device. We do not use push notifications or any external notification servers.

## 8. Bug Reports / External Services
If you choose to use the "Report a Bug" feature, the App will open a standard web browser pointing to an external Google Form. Any information you enter into that form is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. The App itself does not automatically transmit logs or crash reports.

## 9. Third-Party Services
We **do not** include third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics or Google Analytics), or crash reporting tools in this App. The only external API used is Open-Meteo for weather, as described above.

## 10. Data Sharing
Since the App does not collect any data, we do not share, sell, or rent any personal information with third parties.

## 11. Data Retention and Deletion
Your data (such as planner tasks and preferences) is retained locally on your device as long as the App is installed. You can delete all your data at any time simply by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's storage in your device settings.

## 12. Children's Privacy
The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If a parent or guardian becomes aware that their child has provided us with information (e.g., via a bug report form), they should contact us.

## 13. Security
We value your trust. Because all your data remains on your device, its security relies on the security of your device itself. We recommend keeping your device updated and secured with a lock screen.

## 14. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since the App does not collect your contact information, we cannot notify you directly. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
